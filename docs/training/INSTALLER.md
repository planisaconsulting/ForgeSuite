# Installer

The phone follows the job. It does not approve an expense while offline.

1. Open the field pack for the installation.
2. Record travel, arrival, the checklist, photos, snags, and the customer’s name at sign-off.
3. A blocking snag stops completion.
4. For silicone, parking, or a toll, capture the receipt, the amount, and the job at Expenses. Submit it. A supervisor approves it. That approval can add the cost to the job once. It does not pay you.
5. If the network drops, keep the draft. Sync when you are back. Do not send the same draft as a second expense.

See `docs/INSTALLATION_FIELD_WORK.md` and `docs/EXPENSES.md`.
