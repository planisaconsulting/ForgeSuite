# Sales

Create the customer, then the quote. Today’s price list does not change a quote you already saved.

1. Add the customer and a contact.
2. Take a messy enquiry at Sales intake if you want a proposed structure. Confirm the customer, the sizes, and the product yourself. The message does not set the price.
3. Build the estimate and the quote. Check width, height, material, waste, cost, sell, VAT, and margin.
4. Send the quote. A revision keeps the previous revision.
5. When the customer accepts, convert that revision to a job.

See `docs/USER_GUIDE.md` and `docs/SALES_INTAKE.md`.
