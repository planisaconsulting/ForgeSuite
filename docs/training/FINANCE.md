# Finance

An invoice balance is the invoice total less recorded payments and credits. Cash collected is not the same figure as revenue.

1. Issue the deposit, progress, or final invoice from the accepted work. VAT stays on the document as it was issued.
2. Record a payment and allocate it. A payment that is too large stays as unallocated credit. It does not vanish into the invoice.
3. Reverse a wrong payment. The original row stays.
4. A credit note changes the balance. It is not a deleted invoice.
5. The statement and the ageing report read those same rows.
6. An approved expense is a job cost. Exporting a reimbursement batch does not pay the person.
