# Management

Project value, invoiced amounts, and cash collected are three different numbers.

1. Use the dashboard for the current picture, then open the job or project that a figure came from.
2. A project total includes its jobs’ actual cost once. It does not add that same cost again as a separate project expense.
3. Job profitability should show material, labour, contractor, logistics, and approved expenses, each with a source.
4. Approvals sit with the person who has the permission. You do not approve your own expense when the category forbids it.
5. Reports are filters and totals. They do not rank people by what they spent.

See `docs/PROJECTS_AND_ROLLOUTS.md` and `docs/USER_GUIDE.md`.
