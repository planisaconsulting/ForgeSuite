# UAT checklist

Mark each line Pass or Fail, with the person’s name and the date. Use a training copy or a pilot job, not a made-up total typed into the database.

## Sales

- [ ] Create a lead and a customer
- [ ] Create an opportunity, an estimate, and a quote
- [ ] Revise the quote and confirm the old total is unchanged
- [ ] Send or preview the PDF
- [ ] Accept the quote and convert it to a job
- [ ] Decline a different quote with a lost reason and confirm no job exists

## Workshop

- [ ] Open My work and a job card
- [ ] Confirm the card shows the approved artwork revision
- [ ] Scan and issue material once
- [ ] Submit the same issue again and confirm stock did not move twice
- [ ] Start, pause, and complete a stage
- [ ] Fail QC, reprint, and pass QC
- [ ] Confirm the job cost includes the reprint material

## Installer

- [ ] Download a field pack
- [ ] Open the map link for the site
- [ ] Complete a checklist item
- [ ] Add a photo and a snag
- [ ] Capture a signature
- [ ] Sync and confirm the office record updated once

## Finance

- [ ] Issue a deposit invoice and record the deposit
- [ ] Issue the final invoice and confirm the deposit was not invoiced again on top of the full job
- [ ] Allocate one payment across two invoices
- [ ] Record an overpayment and confirm customer credit, not a negative invoice
- [ ] Issue a partial credit note and read the statement

## Purchasing

- [ ] Raise a request and a purchase order
- [ ] Receive part of the order
- [ ] Receive the rest and confirm the order completes
- [ ] Confirm stock and the supplier cost snapshot

## Management

- [ ] Open the dashboard, a profitability view you are allowed to see, backlog, and cash
- [ ] Open MRP and capacity
- [ ] Approve or reject a queued approval
- [ ] Confirm a user without costing permission cannot open the costing URL

## Admin

- [ ] Create a user and force a password change
- [ ] Change a role and read the audit row
- [ ] Take a backup
- [ ] Open system health and confirm cron and backup times
- [ ] Revoke a test device and confirm the next sync is refused
